Physiotherapy Care

Learn how to prepare for your appointment and discuss your mobility, recovery, and treatment goals with your physiotherapist.

Bring Medical Records

Carry recent medical reports, scans, and medication lists so your therapist can plan treatment effectively.

Wear Comfortable Clothing

Choose loose, breathable clothes that allow easy movement and let the therapist examine the affected area.

Note Your Symptoms

Write down pain type, frequency, triggers, and relief methods to help your therapist assess your condition.

Set Clear Goals

Decide on short- and long-term recovery goals so your therapist can tailor sessions to your needs.

Arrive Early

Reach a few minutes early to complete forms, settle in, and prepare mentally for the session.

Ask Questions

Discuss your concerns, exercises, and recovery plan openly to get the most from each appointment.
